# Nabta Kids (نبتة): learning apps for Muslim children

Nabta Kids makes learning apps for children aged 3 to 7. The apps teach the Quran, Tajweed, Arabic, critical thinking and math, and help children's minds grow. They have no ads, collect no data and work offline.

The website is `index.html`. It is in Arabic and English, and the button at the top switches between them. Add `?lang=en` to the address to open it in English.

## What's on the page
- **About us**: the company story, mission, vision and values
- **The program**: the six learning areas
- **How we make our apps**: the four-step process
- **Our apps**: Quran Words (ready to download for Android) and three apps in development
- **Schools and partners**: for schools, Quran centres, supporters and investors
- **Contact**: support, partnership and feedback buttons

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Push this repository to GitHub as a public repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, and press **Save**.
3. After a minute the site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## Add a "Buy me a coffee" link
Once you have a Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi or PayPal.me page, open `index.html`, find `const COFFEE_LINK = '';` near the end, and put your link between the quotes. Until then, the coffee button opens an email.
