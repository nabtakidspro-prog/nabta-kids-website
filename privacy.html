Privacy policy for Nabtawords

Nabtawords (كلماتي من القرآن) is a learning app for children aged 3 to 7, made by Nabta Kids. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

باختصار: لا نجمع أي معلومات عن طفلك أو عنك. لا حسابات، ولا إعلانات، ولا أدوات تتبّع. كل ما يحفظه التطبيق، مثل التقدّم واسم الطفل والرسومات، يبقى على الجهاز فقط. وإذا اخترتَ دعمنا، فإن Google Play هي التي تتولّى الدفع، ولا نرى بيانات بطاقتك.

In short: we do not collect any information about your child or you. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything the app keeps, like progress, the child's name and their pictures, stays on the device. If you choose to support us, Google Play handles the payment and we never see your card details.

What the app keeps, and where

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Progress (games, stories, letters and words finished, stickers), minutes played each dayTo show the child's stickers and the grown-ups' progress reportOnly on the device
The child's first name, if a grown-up types it in SettingsSo the child can learn to write their nameOnly on the device
Coloured pictures and drawingsFor the child's gallery in the appOnly on the device
Settings (sound, English help, voice recording on or off)To remember the grown-ups' choicesOnly on the device

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or holding "reset progress" in the grown-ups area, deletes it.

The microphone

In the "Meet" game, the child can say a word and then hear their own voice. The app asks the phone for permission before using the microphone. The recording lasts a few seconds, is played back once or twice, and is deleted when the child moves on. It is never saved or sent anywhere. A grown-up can turn this off in Settings.

Saving pictures to the phone

A grown-up can save a finished picture to the phone's Photos. It is saved only when the button is pressed, and only to the phone.

Supporting us through Google Play

In the grown-ups area, behind a press-and-hold lock, a parent can choose to give a small thank-you through Google Play. It unlocks nothing, and children never need to buy anything to keep playing. Google Play processes the payment under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your name, email or card details. The app uses an internet connection only to talk to Google Play for this. All learning works offline.

What we never do

  • No ads, and no ad or analytics tools.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, camera or other personal data.
  • No sharing or selling of any data, because we collect none.
  • Links that leave the app, such as an email to us, are only in the grown-ups area, behind the press-and-hold lock.

Children's privacy

Nabtawords is made for young children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to share with third parties. This is in line with children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States), GDPR-K (European Union) and PIPEDA (Canada).

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.
